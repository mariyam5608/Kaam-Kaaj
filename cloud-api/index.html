Two sides of Hyderabad's market.

Designed specifically for how employers and workers already communicate every day on their phones.

For Employers

Post a gig in four fields

  • 01 Send a natural message: Type or voice message what you need (e.g. "do mazdoor chahiye qasimabad me, Rs. 1,000 dihari").
  • 02 Automatic extraction: The AI parses role, area, hours, and fare in Pakistani Rupees into your listing.
  • 03 Direct phone notification: As soon as a worker applies, receive their number immediately.
For Workers

Find work without a resume

  • 01 Voice or text registration: Send a quick voice note with your name, area, and skills.
  • 02 Instant job listings: Request active gigs anytime with simple prompts like "jobs", "kaam", or "dihari".
  • 03 One-message apply: Reply with "apply 1" or "#1" to put your phone number in front of the employer.

Core capabilities

Built to solve real language and accessibility hurdles in Pakistan's informal labour economy.

01

Urdu Voice Notes

Transcribed using OpenAI Whisper so workers who cannot read or write English can register and apply naturally.

02

Frictionless Mirroring

Speaks back in Roman Urdu, Urdu script, or English automatically without requiring menu selections or settings.

03

Zero Paperwork

No accounts, no portals, and no CV writing. Both sides reach a direct phone call in under two minutes.

04

Anti-Devanagari Guard

Carefully prompted and temperature-controlled to prevent language models from drifting into Hindi or Devanagari.

Architecture & persistence

Two distinct backends tailored for different operational and deployment environments.

Vercel Serverless (Meta Cloud API)

Zero-state serverless HTTP endpoint live at kaamkaaj-whatsapp.vercel.app. Handles webhooks signed with HMAC-SHA256 from Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API.

Webhook: /webhook

Baileys Persistent Gateway

Direct WebSocket connection for local phone numbers. Manages real-time voice media downloads, Whisper audio decoding, and instant short-circuit rule execution.

Persistence: Supabase PostgreSQL
